Overview

Qualify, route and follow up — without the manual triage

Built for sales and revenue operations teams that receive inbound leads from web forms, campaigns or ads, the product automates the repetitive first pass of lead handling so reps can focus on the leads that matter.

Blended qualification

Deterministic scoring rules are combined with an AI assessment. If the AI is unavailable, the product automatically falls back to the rules engine.

Clear verdict & next action

Each lead receives a qualified, nurture or disqualified decision and a concrete recommended next step for the sales team.

CRM-ready record

A structured contact, company and qualification record is assembled from the latest result, ready to enrich a CRM.

Alerts & speed-to-lead reply

Every outcome is logged to a sales notification channel, and qualified leads get an instant follow-up email prepared automatically.

How it works

The end-to-end lead workflow

Seven automated stages take a lead from first submission to a sales-ready outcome.

  1. 1

    Lead submitted

    The lead is captured with contact, company, budget, timeline and their message.

  2. 2

    Qualification

    Deterministic rules score the profile; an AI assessment is blended in when available.

  3. 3

    Scoring

    Every scoring contribution is itemized and totaled into a transparent 0–100 score.

  4. 4

    Recommended action

    The decision maps to a concrete next step — follow up, nurture, or disqualify.

  5. 5

    CRM update

    A structured, CRM-ready record is built from the latest qualification.

  6. 6

    Sales notification

    The outcome is recorded and pushed to the configured sales alert channel.

  7. 7

    Follow-up email

    Qualified leads get a speed-to-lead reply prepared automatically (dry-run here).
